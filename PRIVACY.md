# Privacy

The canonical privacy policy is published at **https://rjach.github.io/dnd-anywhere/privacy/**. Its source is [`site/src/pages/privacy.md`](site/src/pages/privacy.md), so every change to it is reviewed and visible in this repository's history.

In short:

- DnD Anywhere **does not collect, store, transmit or sell** any personal data.
- The extension makes **no network requests**. CI enforces this on every build.
- When you drag files into a page, it reads the page's structure **on your device** to find upload fields. It never reads or records page content.
- Files you drop go **only to the upload field you drop them on**, as if you'd picked them in the file dialog. The extension doesn't read, copy or send their contents.
- It stores only your **settings** (in `storage.sync`, which your browser may sync to your own account).
- The website uses **no cookies and no analytics**.
