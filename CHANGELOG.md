## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

### Features

- Drag and drop files onto any file input, including hidden inputs behind labels and buttons.
- Drop onto upload buttons that create their file input on click (trigger capture).
- Upload fields inside open and closed shadow roots and iframes.
- Respects `accept` and `multiple`; steps aside on sites with their own drag and drop (hold Alt to override).
- Toolbar popup with per-site switch, site reporting and diagnostic report.
- Options page, welcome page with a live demo, English localization.
